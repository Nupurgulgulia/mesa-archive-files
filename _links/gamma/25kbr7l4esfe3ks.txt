BANT
Budget

Evaluates the prospect's financial readiness and potential to invest in the solution

Authority

Confirms the lead's power to make or significantly influence purchasing decisions

Need

Identifies how closely the lead's challenges match the product or service's core capabilities

Timeline

Gauges the prospect's urgency and expected decision-making window

While BANT provides a structured approach to lead qualification, it has limitations in capturing the nuanced landscape of modern B2B sales.

Progressive organizations now complement BANT with more holistic lead scoring techniques to ensure a comprehensive and adaptive qualification process.
CHAMP
Challenges

Explore specific pain points and obstacles confronting potential customers

Authority

Assess organizational decision-making dynamics and key stakeholders

Money

Evaluate financial resources and investment potential

Prioritization

Understand strategic priorities and potential impact

CHAMP explores a lead's unique context, including pain points, obstacles, decision-making dynamics, and strategic priorities. This enables crafting targeted engagement strategies.

CHAMP recognizes complex buyer journeys, prioritizing comprehensive lead understanding to build genuine connections, demonstrate value, and develop tailored solutions.
GPCT BA/C&I Framework
Goals

Involve understanding the lead's objectives and what they aim to achieve with a product or service

Plans

Refer to the strategies they have in place to reach those goals

Challenges

Help identify the obstacles they face

Timeline

Gauges their urgency in making a decision

Budget

Assesses their financial capacity

Authority

Determines if they have decision-making power

Negative Consequences

Examine the risks of not addressing their challenges

Positive Implications/Critical Events

Explore the potential benefits of adopting the offering

This strategy goes beyond BANT by incorporating qualitative factors and focuses on building a deeper understanding of the lead's needs and motivations.

As the buyer-seller dynamic evolves, GPCTBA/C&I provides a modern approach to lead qualification, reflecting the complexities of the buyer's journey and fostering a customer-centric sales process.
MEDDICC
Metrics

Involve quantifiable measures that determine the potential value of the solution to the lead's business

Economic Buyer

Refers to identifying the person with the authority to make purchasing decisions

Decision Criteria

Entails understanding the specific factors that influence the lead's decision-making process

Decision Process

Involves mapping out the steps and stakeholders involved in the lead's buying journey

Identify Pain

Focuses on uncovering the lead's pain points and challenges

Champion

Pertains to finding an internal advocate who champions the solution within the lead's organization

Competition

What's the alternative or the competition?

MEDDIC helps sales teams gather comprehensive insights into a lead's needs, motivations, and decision-making dynamics, enabling tailored engagement strategies.

MEDDIC aids in building strong relationships, mitigating obstacles, and aligning solutions with the lead's unique requirements, increasing conversion and customer satisfaction.

Company size
Annual revenue thresholds
Number of employees
Technical requirements

Subjective Criteria
NO ONE wants to sit and answer questions
Ask Strategic Questions - "Start broad, then narrow down"
Sample Broad Questions:
'What prompted you to look into this now?'
'What could be better about the current solution?'
'Who else is involved in making this decision?'
'What happens if you don't solve this problem?'
'What's your timeline for implementation?'
Use the 'Why? What? How?' technique:
Why is this important?
What impact does it have?
How urgent is the solution needed?
Identify both:
Current problems (immediate needs)
Anticipated challenges (future needs)
