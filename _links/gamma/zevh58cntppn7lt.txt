The Core Idea of JTBD
People don't buy products. They hire them to get a job done.

Customers choose products because they help them make progress in a situation. JTBD shifts focus from customer profiles to customer intent.

Hire Uber

To reach somewhere reliably

Hire Spotify

To improve a moment

Hire Excel

To analyze numbers

Hire Coffee

To stay alert

Hire ChatGPT

To think faster

Hire Amazon

To get the best selection & deal 

The product is a tool. The real value is the progress created. Better solutions win by helping users move forward faster, easier, cheaper, or with less anxiety.

Demographics explain who buys. JTBD explains why they buy — often revealing hidden competitors and growth opportunities.

The Three Layers of a Job

Every job has three distinct layers that must all be understood to design a winning solution.

1
Social Job

How do they want to be perceived?

2
Emotional Job

How does the customer want to feel?

3
Functional Job

What task needs to be completed?

Many products win emotionally or socially, not just functionally. Understanding all three layers is the key to differentiation.

Example: Buying a Laptop

A single purchase decision carries all three job layers simultaneously. Here's how they play out:

⚙️ Functional

Run work apps smoothly without lag or crashes

❤️ Emotional

Feel confident it won't fail during critical moments

👥 Social

Look professional and credible in meetings

Many products win emotionally or socially, not just functionally. Ignoring these layers leaves value on the table.

A Good JTBD Statement

When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [desired outcome].

A well-formed JTBD statement always includes context, motivation, and a success outcome — and never mentions your product.

Commuter

When I commute, I want to learn something useful, so I can use time productively.

New Hire

When I start a new role, I want to understand workflows quickly, so I can contribute fast.

Marketer

When I run ads, I want to know which creatives work, so I can reduce CAC.

Host

When guests visit, I want my home cleaned quickly, so I can feel prepared.

How to Discover Jobs
Interview for behavior, not opinions

Focus on recent switching moments. Real behavior is better than hypothetical answers.

1
What happened before you looked for a solution?

Uncover the triggering situation and context

2
What frustrated you? What alternatives did you try?

Identify existing poor solutions and pain points

3
Why did you choose this option? What nearly stopped you?

Reveal decision criteria and barriers to switching

4
What does success look like? What trade-offs did you make?

Define the desired outcome and acceptable compromises

1

2

Happens often or matters greatly

3

4
Measurable Outcome

5
Urgency & Willingness to Pay

Customers are motivated to act

6
Broad Market & Multiple Improvements

Enough scale and room to innovate

Introducing AMUSED

JTBD identifies demand. AMUSED identifies opportunity. Use it to test if a job is worth solving and where the biggest leverage lies.

1
A — Automate
2
M — Minimize
3
U — Upgrade
4
S — Simplify
5
E — Eliminate
6
D — Delegate

Together, JTBD + AMUSED form a complete system: identify what customers need, then find the best way to deliver it better than anyone else.

AMUSED Explained
A — Automate

Can repetitive or manual work be done automatically? Reduce human effort through technology.

M — Minimize

Can time, effort, cost, or steps be reduced? Compress the job to its essential core.

U — Upgrade

Can quality, speed, confidence, or outcomes improve? Raise the ceiling of what's possible.

S — Simplify

Can the experience become easier? Lower the skill or effort required to succeed.

E — Eliminate

Can friction or waste disappear entirely? Remove steps that don't add value.

D — Delegate

Can parts be handled by software, experts, or others? Shift the burden away from the user.

Example — Expense Management

When month-end closes approach, I want to reconcile employee expenses quickly, so I can close books accurately.

AMUSED Analysis
A: Auto-read receipts
M: Reduce manual entry
U: Better audit accuracy
S: One-click approvals
E: Remove paper receipts
D: Auto-route to managers
Why This Job Is Strong
Recurring pain every month
Quantifiable ROI
Clear budget owner
Operational urgency

All 6 AMUSED levers apply — a strong signal of high opportunity.
