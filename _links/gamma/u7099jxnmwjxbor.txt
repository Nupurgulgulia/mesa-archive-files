Mapping Your Customer's Journey: A Startup Guide

40-60% of deals end in "no decision" despite purchase intent. This presentation will outline a simple framework for mapping your customer's journey and improving your startup's success.

Last edited 29 days ago

Understanding the Buyer Journey
Definition

The path customers take from realizing a need to making a purchase.

Framework Example

Trigger → Learn → Try → Buy

Buyers care about their problems and not your sales process
The Impact on Your Business
Key Points
Reduces "no decision" outcomes
Helps create targeted content
Improves sales conversion
Builds better customer relationships
Building Your Journey Map
Buyer Stages

Identify key stages like awareness, consideration, decision, and purchase.

Customer Emotions

Understand how customers feel at each stage, such as excitement, frustration, or confusion.

Key Questions

List questions customers ask themselves and needs they seek to fulfill.

Touch Points

Identify interactions customers have with your brand, such as website visits, social media posts, or email campaigns.

Pain Points

Document challenges or obstacles customers encounter throughout their journey.

Practical Framework for Startups
1
1. Identify Target Customer

Who are you trying to reach? Define your ideal customer profile.

2
2. Map Key Stages

Break down the journey into distinct phases based on your target customer.

3
3. List Questions/Needs

What questions does your customer ask at each stage? What are their needs and goals?

4
4. Document Pain Points

What challenges do customers face at each stage? How can you address them?

5
5. Plan Content/Actions

Create content and strategies to guide customers through the journey.
