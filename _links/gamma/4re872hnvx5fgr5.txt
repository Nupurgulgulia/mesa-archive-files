Best Practices for Writing Effective Cold Outreach

Last edited 2 months ago

Why Most Cold Outreach Sucks
Irrelevant

Sending generic messages to everyone, without tailoring them to the recipient's needs.

Unpersonalized

Failing to address the recipient by name or mention anything specific about their company or role.

Examples of Good vs Bad Outreach
Good

Subject: Excited About Your [Company Name] Growth!

Body: Hi [Recipient Name], I've been following [Company Name]'s success in [Industry] and was particularly impressed with your recent [Achievement]. ...

Bad

Subject: Check out our amazing service!

Body: Dear Sir/Madam, We offer the best [Service Name] in the market. ...

Crafting an Attention-Grabbing Subject Line
1
Keep it Short & Sweet

Aim for under 6 words.

2
Use Curiosity & Intrigue

Ask a question or tease a benefit.

3
Personalize Where Possible

Mention their name or company.

Personalizing Your Message
Research

Take time to learn about their company and goals.

Connect

Mention something specific you saw online or in their work.

Engage

Start with a genuine compliment or question.

Principles of Effective Outreach
1
Give Value First

Lead with what you can offer them

2
Focus on Outcomes

Emphasize results over methods

3
Highlight Benefits

Show impact, not features

4
Solve Problems

Address their specific challenges

5
Keep It Simple

Use clear language and be concise

These foundational principles build upon each other to create effective cold outreach that resonates with recipients and drives results.

Optimizing for Mobile Readability
1
Short Paragraphs

Use 1-2 sentences per paragraph.

2
Bullet Points

Break up long text with bullet points.

3
Strong Calls to Action

Make it clear what you want them to do.

Closing with a Clear Call-to-Action
1
Direct

Tell them what to do.

2
Specific

Include a link to a website or schedule a call.

3
Timely

Provide a deadline or incentive.

Workshop

Relevancy: Highlight why you’re reaching out specifically.

Problem Statement: Twist the knife with comparisons/stats.
Dream Outcome: Calculate how they reach their ideal state.
