Copy of Personas

Personas

Personas are fictional representations of the ideal customer. They are more specific than the target audience and contain comprehensive details about who the buyer is, defining who the company wants to sell to and who their ideal clients are. Understanding personas is key to building solid relationships with customers.

Last edited 3 months ago

Why care about Personas?
1
Comprehensive Details

A buyer persona contains comprehensive details of who your buyer is.

2
Defines Your Company

It defines who your company is and who you want to sell to.

3
Ideal Clients

It defines who your ideal clients are and what you need to know about them in order to build a solid relationship with them.

4
Multiple Personas

A business can have one target audience and multiple buyer personas.

Refine

Continuously refine and update the personas as you gather more information.
