TROUBLESHOOTING 101
.
Spot the signs, run the play.

ACTION PLAN ACTION PLAN ACTION PLAN
1. Request a specific completion date. 1. Document with detailed photos immediately. 1. Switch channels — call, then WhatsApp.
2. Set daily check-ins via phone/WhatsApp. 2. Contact supplier within 24h with evidence. 2. Restate your order timeline and intent to buy.
3. Consider partial shipments 3. Request partial refund or replacement. 3. Give a firm 48-hour deadline to respond.
